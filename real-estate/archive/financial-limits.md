# Max PITI (Assuming no rental income)
- Comfortable: $2450/mo (28% of gross income)
- Reasonable: $2800/mo (32% of gross income)
- Manageable: $3060/mo (35% of gross income)
- Tight: $3280/mo (37.5% of gross income)
- Aggresive: $3500/mo (40% of gross income)
