# Green Flags
- They distinguish between legal units and "looks like a unit." For example, they don't assume a finished basement apartment is legal just because there's a kitchen in it.
- They're willing to obtain and review documents such as rent rolls, leases, utility bills, tax records, COs, zoning information, and seller expense history.
- They are comfortable telling me, "This property doesn't make sense at this price". Avoid someone whose solution to every property is "write an offer".
# Red Flags
