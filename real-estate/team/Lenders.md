-And lastly, use a local lender! Stay away from big banks and internet lenders; they are terrible and local lenders are the best. You can get a pre-approval from a local lender (hopefully your agent has a great recommendation) and he will be able to run the numbers for you to see how much you can afford and work with your monthly mortgage payments to ensure you're comfortable with them.
-It's best to go with the lender your agent recommends because she has probably worked with this lender for years and knows she can count on him and get the job done with out any head aches (and NO, an agent is not getting a kickback from the lender if you use the recommended lender. This is totally illegal). Just make sure she recommends a local mortgage lender. Banks and internet lenders are horrible to get mortgages from. Good luck!!
